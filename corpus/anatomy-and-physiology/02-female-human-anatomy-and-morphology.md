# Female Human Anatomy and Morphological Landmarks

A comprehensive structural guide to the female human anatomy, skeletal dimorphism,
surface contours, mammary complexes, pelvic architecture, and external reproductive landmarks.

## 1. Craniofacial and Cervical Features

- **Neurocranium & Forehead**: Smoother frontal bone curvature, vertical forehead ascent, less prominent supraorbital torus, delicate orbital rims.
- **Midface & Jawline**: Softer malar fat pad projection, obtuse gonial angle of mandible (~120-125°), narrower, more tapered mentum (chin).
- **Cervical (Neck)**: Slender cylindrical contour; thyroid cartilage laminae meet at an obtuse angle (~120°), yielding an unprojected, smooth anterior neck without a prominent Adam's apple.

## 2. Thorax, Mammary Glands, and Breasts

- **Skeletal Thorax**: Shorter sternum body relative to manubrium, wider subcostal angle (>75°), narrower transverse rib cage diameter relative to pelvic breadth.
- **Mammary Gland Location & Base**: Extends vertically from the 2nd rib down to the 6th rib, and horizontally from the lateral sternal border to the midaxillary line. The glandular tissue rests directly on the pectoral fascia overlying pectoralis major and serratus anterior.
- **Inframammary Fold (IMF)**: An anatomical crease formed by dense dermal collagenous adherence to the 6th rib perichondrium, demarcating the inferior hemispherical boundary of the breast.
- **Areola and Nipple (Papilla Mammaria)**:
  - Typically projects anteriorly over the 4th intercostal space slightly inferior and lateral to the breast hemisphere apex.
  - Areola is a pigmented circular cutaneous zone (~30-50 mm diameter) bearing sebaceous Montgomery tubercles (glandulae areolares).
  - Nipple contains smooth muscle fibers that contract upon sympathetic stimulation, projecting 4-8 mm and terminating in 15-20 microscopic lactiferous duct orifices.
- **Axillary Tail of Spence**: The superolateral wedge of glandular breast tissue extending upward through deep fascia into the axillary fossa.
- **Intermammary Cleft**: Sternal midline trough between medial borders of right and left breasts, creating the cleavage contour.

## 3. Abdominal and Trunk Contour

- **Adipose Distribution**: Subcutaneous adipose tissue is distributed more evenly and thicker over the hypogastric region, hips, and flanks (gynoid pattern under estrogenic regulation).
- **Waist Indentation**: Waist narrowing occurs higher on the torso, superior to the umbilicus at approximately the 10th rib level, producing an hourglass silhouette.
- **Umbilicus**: Centered at L3-L4 vertebral level, flanked by softer rectus abdominis contours with lower muscular definition than male counterparts.

## 4. Gynecoid Pelvis and Hip Framework

- **Pelvic Skeleton**:
  - Pelvic inlet is oval/round with generous anteroposterior diameter.
  - Pelvic cavity is shallow and broad; iliac wings are flared and widely spaced.
  - Subpubic angle / pubic arch is wide, obtuse (>80-85°).
  - Greater sciatic notch is broad and shallow.
- **Hip-to-Waist Proportions**: Wider bitrochanteric and bicristal widths, creating a classic waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) of approximately 0.7.
- **Femoral Q-Angle**: Greater quadriceps angle (~17° measured from ASIS to patella center to tibial tubercle), influencing lower extremity alignment and pelvic width.

## 5. Female External Genitalia (Vulva & Perineum)

- **Mons Pubis**: A rounded subcutaneous adipose eminence positioned anteriorly over the pubic symphysis, typically covered with post-pubertal hair tapering laterally in a triangular pattern.
- **Labia Majora**: Bilateral, longitudinal cutaneous folds extending posteriorly from the mons pubis to the perineal body; contain subcutaneous fat, sweat glands, and homologous tissue to the male scrotum.
- **Labia Minora**: Paired thin, hairless cutaneous/mucosal folds located medial to the labia majora, flanking the vaginal vestibule. Superiorly, they divide to form the prepuce (hood) and frenulum of the clitoris.
- **Clitoris**: Highly vascularized, erectile organ:
  - Glans clitoris: Sensitive distal tubercle visible beneath the clitoral hood.
  - Body and paired crura: Extend posteriorly along the ischiopubic rami.
- **Vaginal Vestibule**: The cleft between labia minora containing:
  - External urethral orifice: Located approximately 2.5 cm inferior to the clitoris, anterior to the vaginal opening.
  - Introitus / Vaginal orifice: Caudal aperture of the vaginal canal, flanked by the greater vestibular (Bartholin's) glands.
- **Perineal Body & Perineum**: Fibromuscular node centered between the posterior labial commissure and the anterior verge of the anus.

## 6. Dorsal and Gluteal Region

- **Lumbar Spine**: Distinct lordotic curvature with greater lumbosacral angle.
- **Posterior Superior Iliac Spines (PSIS)**: Cutaneous depressions (dimples of Venus) marking bilateral S2 sacral attachments.
- **Gluteal Region**: Full, rounded contour formed by gluteus maximus, gluteus medius, and substantial subcutaneous adipose cushion; defined infragluteal crease and vertical natal cleft.
