# Male Human Anatomy and Morphological Landmarks

A comprehensive structural guide to male human anatomy, skeletal dimorphism,
surface contours, pectoral musculature, android pelvic architecture, and external reproductive landmarks.

## 1. Craniofacial and Cervical Features

- **Neurocranium & Forehead**: Larger robust cranial vault, sloped forehead profile, prominent supraorbital ridges, pronounced glabella, and heavier zygomatic arches.
- **Midface & Mandible**: Squarer, wider mental protuberance (chin), acute gonial angle of the mandible (~110-115°), visible masseter muscle bulk, and presence of terminal facial hair follicles across jawline, cheeks, and upper lip.
- **Cervical (Neck) & Laryngeal Prominence**:
  - Heavier muscular circumference with distinct sternocleidomastoid and trapezius outlines.
  - Laryngeal Prominence (Adam's Apple / Pomum Adami): Thyroid cartilage laminae meet at an acute angle (~90°), creating a prominent anterior protrusion midway down the anterior cervical midline, superior to the cricoid cartilage and jugular notch.

## 2. Thorax and Pectoral Region

- **Skeletal Thorax**: Heavier, broader thoracic cage with greater anteroposterior and transverse diameters; longer sternal body (exceeding twice manubrium length); acute subcostal angle (~70°).
- **Pectoralis Major Musculature**:
  - Heavy muscular chest wall consisting of clavicular, sternocostal, and abdominal heads.
  - Sternal margin is sharply delineated along the midline; lower pectoral border forms a crisp transverse boundary.
  - Lateral border forms the anterior axillary fold over the axillary vault.
  - Serratus anterior digitations are visible along lateral ribs 5 through 8.
- **Male Nipple and Areola**:
  - Located over the 4th intercostal space, approximately 10-12 cm from the sternal midline.
  - Smaller areolar diameter (~20-28 mm) with flat or subtly raised conical papilla.
  - Rests directly on the pectoral fascia without glandular projection under typical physiological conditions.

## 3. Abdominal Wall and Muscular Definition

- **Rectus Abdominis**: Bounded laterally by the curved linea semilunaris and divided medially by the fibrous linea alba.
- **Tendinous Intersections**: 3 to 4 horizontal fibrous bands segmenting rectus abdominis into discrete muscular bellies ("six-pack" architecture).
- **Waist and Flank Contour**: Lower waist indentation sitting immediately above the iliac crests; muscular external oblique ridges creating an athletic V-taper tapering from broad biacromial shoulders down to narrower hips.
- **Inguinal Ligament & Groin Crease**: Prominent linear surface crease extending from the Anterior Superior Iliac Spine (ASIS) to the pubic tubercle.

## 4. Android Pelvis and Hip Framework

- **Pelvic Skeleton**:
  - Heavier, thicker bone construction with marked muscular attachment rugosities.
  - Pelvic inlet is heart-shaped (android morphology); pelvic cavity is deep, narrow, and funnel-shaped.
  - Subpubic angle / pubic arch is narrow and acute (<70°), resembling an inverted V.
  - Greater sciatic notch is deep and narrow.
- **Hip-to-Shoulder Proportions**: Narrower bitrochanteric width relative to biacromial shoulder breadth; waist-to-hip ratio (WHR) typically ranges between 0.85 and 0.95.
- **Femoral Q-Angle**: Smaller quadriceps angle (~12°), reflecting narrower pelvic width and straighter femoral descent to the patella.

## 5. Male External Genitalia (Penis & Scrotum)

- **Mons Pubis**: Flatter, narrower pubic eminence with escutcheon hair pattern extending superiorly in an inverted triangle toward the umbilicus along the linea alba.
- **Penis**: External copulatory and urinary organ situated anterior to the scrotum at the subpubic angle:
  - Root (radix): Fixed proximal attachment consisting of paired crura attached to the ischiopubic rami and the central bulb of the penis attached to the perineal membrane.
  - Body / Shaft (corpus): Cylindrical mobile portion containing three parallel erectile columns enclosed in Buck's deep fascia: bilateral dorsal corpora cavernosa and the ventral corpus spongiosum (transmitting the spongy urethra).
  - Glans Penis: Distal conical expansion of the corpus spongiosum capping the blunt ends of the corpora cavernosa; features the corona glandis (prominent proximal rim), retroglandular sulcus, external urethral orifice (vertical slit at the apex), and prepuce (foreskin).
- **Scrotum**:
  - Cutaneous fibromuscular sac suspended inferior to the pubic symphysis, containing the paired testes, epididymides, and distal spermatic cords.
  - Divided externally by the median scrotal raphe, which continues anteriorly onto the ventral penile shaft and posteriorly across the perineum to the anus.
  - Dartos muscle in subcutaneous tissue regulates testicular temperature through scrotal contraction and relaxation.
- **Perineum & Perineal Raphe**: Midline surface ridge extending from the posterior base of the scrotum to the anterior anal margin.

## 6. Dorsal, Scapular, and Gluteal Region

- **Upper Back & Scapulae**: Broad biacromial span with pronounced trapezius, rhomboid, infraspinatus, and latissimus dorsi bulk, creating a distinct triangular silhouette.
- **Gluteal Region**: Formed by gluteus maximus and medius with lower subcutaneous adipose padding; presents a firmer, squarer, more muscular contour with shallower infragluteal folds compared to female morphology.
